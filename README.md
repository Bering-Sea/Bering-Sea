# Aleutian

From St. George & St. Paul Island, in the Pribilofs, out in the Bering Sea. Still working there.

The code is not in this account. It is in two organizations, both free.

## [Pribilof Traffic](https://github.com/PribilofTraffic)

[pribiloftraffic.com](https://www.pribiloftraffic.com) watches the sky and the sea around St. Paul and St. George: who is inbound, whether the approach is above minimums, the wind at both stations, and the forecast. The programs are small Docker collectors. They write down what came back, including the times nothing did.


## [Pribilof Hangar](https://github.com/Pribilof-Hangar)

Free MSFS 2024 add-ons for classic airliners, built around the PMDG DC-6. Gauges for Logitech Flight Instrument Panels, a spoken copilot, and the old north-side gravel runway at St. George.

[pribilof.info](https://www.pribilof.info) is the rest of what I build.
